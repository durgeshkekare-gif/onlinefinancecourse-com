IIM Ahmedabad FRCG — What It Is

The Financial Reporting & Corporate Governance (FRCG) programme by IIM Ahmedabad is a 6-month executive certificate covering financial reporting standards (IndAS, IFRS) and corporate governance frameworks. ~₹1.85L + GST. Online with IIM-A campus immersion. Gives IIM Ahmedabad alumni status. IIM-A is Triple Crown accredited (AACSB + AMBA + EQUIS) and ranked #1 in India by NIRF consistently.

Who Is FRCG For?

The programme is specifically designed for: CAs, CFAs and CPAs targeting CFO, VP Finance or board roles; finance directors and controllers who need governance frameworks; audit committee members who need financial reporting depth; and finance professionals who want IIMA credentials at the most accessible fee point (~₹1.85L is among the lowest IIMA programme fees).

What FRCG Covers

  • IndAS (Indian Accounting Standards) — full coverage
  • IFRS — international financial reporting, convergence with IndAS
  • Corporate governance frameworks — board structure, audit committees, SEBI LODR
  • Director responsibilities — legal liability, fiduciary duties
  • Financial analysis and valuation using IFRS/IndAS data
  • ESG reporting — sustainability disclosures and governance
  • Regulatory compliance — SEBI, MCA, RBI reporting requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee for IIM Ahmedabad FRCG 2026?

~₹1,85,000 excluding GST (18%). Total approximately ₹2.18L all-in. Application fee separately. Verify exact current batch fee on official Jaro Education programme page.

Is IIM Ahmedabad FRCG worth it for CA?

Yes — FRCG is particularly well-suited for CAs who want IIM Ahmedabad credentials. CAs have the technical financial depth; FRCG adds the corporate governance frameworks and IIMA institutional credibility needed for CFO and board-level roles. At ~₹1.85L, it is the most affordable IIMA credential available.

Does IIM Ahmedabad FRCG give alumni status?

Yes — completing the FRCG programme gives IIM Ahmedabad executive alumni status. This is lifetime and includes access to the IIM-A alumni network, events, and institutional association.
