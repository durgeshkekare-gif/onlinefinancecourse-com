IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Batch 1 — What Actually Happened

IIM Nagpur Batch 1 of the Blended MBA for Working Professionals has completed, and the outcomes are now documented. This is one of the most valuable data points available to anyone considering the programme — real professionals, real career outcomes, specific to people who studied while working full-time.

Why Batch 1 Outcomes Matter for You

Most MBA programme claims about career outcomes are based on full-time campus placements. The Blended MBA is different — there are no campus placements. Career impact happens through the credential, the alumni network, and the individual's own career decisions post-completion. Batch 1 outcomes tell you exactly what happens when real working professionals complete this programme.

Who Was in Batch 1?

Batch 1 comprised working professionals from diverse industries — IT, banking, financial services, manufacturing, consulting, healthcare, and government. Entry-level experience ranged from 3 years (the minimum) to 18+ years. The cohort spanned multiple cities with IIM Nagpur campus immersions bringing everyone together physically four times over 2 years.

Finance Professionals in Batch 1

For finance professionals — CAs, CFAs, and banking professionals — the IIM Nagpur MBA added the management credibility that technical qualifications alone lack. The combination of existing finance expertise with IIM institutional brand opened VP Finance, Business Finance Head, and CFO-adjacent roles that were previously out of reach.

Did IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Batch 1 professionals get salary hikes?
